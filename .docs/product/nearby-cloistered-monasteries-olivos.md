# Cloistered Monasteries near Olivos (Vicente López)

> Research doc (2026-09-19). Ordered by distance from Olivos, Vicente López, Zona Norte GBA.
> **Verification levels:** ✅ confirmed active via recent source (2022+) · ⚠️ no reliable online presence found — verify on-site or via the diocese before planning a visit.

## 1. Abadía de Santa Escolástica — Benedictine nuns ✅ CONFIRMED ACTIVE

| | |
|---|---|

| Address | Martín Rodríguez 500, **Victoria, San Fernando** |
| Distance from Olivos | ~8–10 km (15–20 min via Av. del Libertador north) |
| Maps | https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mart%C3%ADn+Rodr%C3%ADguez+500%2C+Victoria%2C+San+Fernando |
| Facebook | https://www.facebook.com/AbadiaSantaEscolastica/ |
| Source | [La Nación, 2022](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/lifestyle/son-35-no-ven-television-ni-usan-redes-sociales-pero-con-una-receta-que-inventaron-entre-todas-nid22122022/) — 35 cloistered nuns, famous pan dulce shop by the abbey church door |

**This is the closest cloistered community to Olivos and the most visitor-friendly.**

## Notes

- Cloistered communities don't receive visitors inside; interaction is via public Mass, the locutorio (parlor grille), and monastery shops.
- The Bethlehem sisters' monastery (Monasterio Santa María en la Santísima Trinidad) is in **Merlo, San Luis** (~800 km) — NOT nearby, despite appearing in initial searches.
- For verified Mass times/contact, call ahead or check the diocese of San Isidro (for zona norte) / Arquidiócesis de Buenos Aires (for CABA).
